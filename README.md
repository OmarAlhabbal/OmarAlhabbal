### What I work on

Most of my work sits on the unglamorous side of analytics: taking data from
sources that don't agree with each other, getting it into one place that
people can trust, and building the reporting or models on top of it.

That has meant migrating 10,000+ records off a crash-prone Excel tracker
into structured SharePoint Lists and building the Power BI layer on top,
consolidating 7,500+ scattered contacts into a single working CRM, and
engineering a 62,631-record dataset from four separate sources for a
predictive modeling project.
